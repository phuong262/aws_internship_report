**Period:** From **29/06/2026** to **04/07/2026**

### Week 2 Objectives

* Study AWS services related to storage, content delivery, databases, scaling, encryption, and user authentication.
* Compare their advantages, limitations, and use cases before selecting the Smart Docs AI architecture.
* Build the foundation needed for document-processing work in the following weeks.

### Week 2 Achievements

* Understood the roles of S3, CloudFront, RDS, Aurora, DynamoDB, Auto Scaling, KMS, and Cognito.
* Understood the overall Smart Docs AI architecture and identified the services requiring deeper study.
* Defined the assigned document-processing responsibility: receive files from S3, invoke Lambda and Textract, and normalize the extracted content.
