This page summarizes the work completed during the eight-week internship (from **22/06/2026** to **15/08/2026**) as part of the First Cloud AI Journey (FCAJ) program. Throughout the internship, the work combined research into foundational AWS services with their direct application in the development of **Smart Docs AI**—an intelligent document question-answering and knowledge extraction platform based on Retrieval-Augmented Generation (RAG) and an AWS serverless architecture.
