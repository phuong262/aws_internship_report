After eight weeks in the **First Cloud AI Journey** program, from **22/06/2026** to **15/08/2026**, I gained practical experience in a professional environment while working with my team to develop the **Smart Docs AI** system on AWS.

In the project, I was responsible for the document-processing stage. My primary responsibilities were configuring an automated Amazon S3 trigger, developing an AWS Lambda function that called Amazon Textract to extract content from PDF files and images, and processing fragmented text blocks into complete document content. The output was then passed to subsequent stages for vector embedding generation, storage, and semantic search.

## Overall Evaluation

### 1. Working Environment

The working environment was comfortable, well maintained, and suitable for focused research and development. Weekly events covered diverse topics and gave me opportunities to gain knowledge and experience beyond my primary project responsibilities.

The shared workspace also allowed me to observe how other teams organized their work, communicated, and solved problems. This was valuable because I learned not only from technical documentation but also from the practical working habits of other interns.

### 2. Support from the Mentor and Team Admin

The mentor provided detailed guidance, explained unclear concepts carefully, and encouraged me to ask questions. Instead of immediately providing an answer, the mentor often guided me to research, experiment, and evaluate possible solutions independently. This approach strengthened my analytical and problem-solving abilities.

The team admin was friendly, responsive, and willing to help with information, documentation, and program-related questions. Timely support from both the mentor and team admin helped me maintain progress and work more confidently with AWS services that were new to me.

### 3. Relevance to My Academic Major

The assigned work was relevant to my Information Technology major and connected with knowledge I had acquired at university. In the **Open Source Software Development** course, I had been introduced to Cloud concepts and several AWS services, which gave me a useful foundation for beginning the internship.

Building a real document-processing pipeline, however, required knowledge beyond the classroom curriculum. I needed to understand Amazon S3 events, AWS Lambda event handling, Amazon Textract APIs, and output normalization. This allowed me to reinforce my programming and data-processing knowledge while expanding my understanding of Serverless architecture on AWS.

### 4. Learning and Skill Development Opportunities

During the internship, I developed teamwork, time-management, and professional communication skills. Our team held weekly meetings to review results, discuss difficulties, and assign tasks for the following week. These meetings helped me understand my individual responsibilities and how my document-processing component connected with the AI/RAG, database, API, and testing components.

From a technical perspective, I gained hands-on experience with Amazon S3, AWS Lambda, and Amazon Textract. I learned how to design an event-driven workflow, validate input data, track processing status, and standardize results for downstream components. I also improved my ability to read AWS documentation, inspect logs, and present technical outcomes in reports.

### 5. Culture and Team Spirit

The workplace culture was positive. Everyone respected one another and worked seriously while maintaining a friendly atmosphere. We shared the workspace with internship teams from several universities. Each person prepared the necessary personal equipment and followed the common office rules.

Although more than 100 people could be working in the office at the same time, the environment remained relatively quiet and suitable for concentration. Within the Smart Docs AI team, members owned different components but regularly discussed data formats and integration points. This reduced conflicts and ensured that my extracted data could be used in the embedding, storage, and question-answering stages.

### 6. Internship Conditions and Support

The program offered flexibility in working hours when necessary, helping me balance university commitments with internship responsibilities. Participating in program events and knowledge-sharing sessions also allowed me to expand my technical knowledge, connect with the community, and better understand possible career paths in Cloud computing.

## Additional Questions

### What Was I Most Satisfied With During the Internship?

I was most satisfied with the opportunity to participate in developing a complete product, from the initial RAG chatbot concept to a functional Smart Docs AI system on AWS. Although each member was responsible for a different component, all components had to work closely together to form a consistent processing workflow.

For my individual contribution, the most memorable experience was building the automated document-processing flow. A file uploaded to Amazon S3 invoked AWS Lambda, Lambda called Amazon Textract to extract the document content, and the result was processed and assembled into complete text. Seeing the data I processed continue into the embedding and semantic-search stages helped me understand the value of each component in the overall architecture.

### What Could the Program Improve for Future Interns?

The program could create more opportunities for interaction among the internship teams sharing the same office. Each team currently focuses primarily on its own project, leaving limited time to learn about the topics, technologies, and working methods of other groups.

Short cross-team presentations could help. Each team could introduce its problem, architecture, and one notable technical challenge. This activity would allow interns to explore different approaches and build stronger connections across the program.

### Would I Recommend This Program to Friends?

Yes. I would recommend the program to students who want to learn about Cloud computing and gain experience contributing to a practical AWS project. Interns can work with services such as Amazon Bedrock, Amazon Textract, AWS Lambda, Amazon S3, Amazon RDS, Amazon DynamoDB, and Amazon CloudWatch as part of a complete architecture.

The mentor provides close support while still giving interns the freedom to research and take responsibility for their work. This helps participants learn new technologies while developing problem-solving and team-collaboration skills within a relatively short period.

## Suggestions and Future Expectations

### Suggestions for Improving the Internship Experience

In addition to technical sessions, the program could include one or two career-oriented talks from professionals with extensive AWS and Cloud experience. Topics could include employer expectations for new graduates, strategies for building a learning roadmap, career directions, and lessons from real projects.

The program could also add a midterm architecture review so that teams receive early feedback on security, scalability, cost, and integration among components.

### Interest in Future Programs

If given the opportunity, I would like to participate in similar First Cloud AI Journey or AWS programs in the future. After gaining more knowledge and experience, I would also be interested in supporting future cohorts by sharing my data-processing experience and helping new interns become familiar with AWS services.

### Acknowledgements

I sincerely thank the First Cloud AI Journey team, my mentor, the team admin, and the Smart Docs AI team members for providing a professional and friendly environment in which I could learn and practice. This internship was a memorable experience and an important foundation for my continued development in Cloud computing and data processing.
